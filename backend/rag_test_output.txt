| Focus Area | Expert Guidance | Important Notes for Farmers |
|---|---|---|
| **Soil & Field Setup** | Plant in well-drained soil with a consistent texture. Dig planting pits that are 45 inches long, 45 inches wide, and 45 cm deep. | Avoid any standing water around the base of the plant to prevent collar rot. Papaya prefers tropical conditions (35-38 °C in summer) and can grow up to 1200 meters elevation. |
| **Watering & Irrigation** | Water your plants generously immediately after planting. Afterward, irrigate once a week. If using a drip system, provide 10 liters of water per plant daily. | Apply 300 grams of Super Phosphate fertilizer to the soil every two months, starting 3-4 Months After Planting (MAP) once plants are thinned. |
| **Leaf Nutrition & Pest Care** | At 4 and 8 Months After Planting, prepare a leaf spray using 0.5% Zinc Sulphate and 0.1% Boron. Spray this mixture thoroughly on the leaves. | This leaf spray helps improve plant growth and fruit yield, and also assists in managing pests. |
| **Variety & Harvesting** | Choose varieties like CO 8, CO 7, CO 5, CO 6, CO 3, CO 2, or Red Lady. Varieties such as CO 2, CO 5, CO 6, and CO 8 are suitable for both eating and papain production. | Papaya plants typically produce fruit for 24-30 months. The ideal time to sow seeds is from June to September. CO 5 and CO 8 are known for high papain yields. |
